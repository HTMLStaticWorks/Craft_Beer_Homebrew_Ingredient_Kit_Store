Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our homebrewing workshops, fresh ingredient shipping, equipment rentals, and shop visits.

We recommend starting with our 1-Gallon All-In-One Starter Kit and taking our Intro to Homebrewing workshop. In just 3 hours, you'll learn sanitization, extract brewing basics, and fermentation control hands-on with a BJCP-certified brewer.
All hops are nitrogen-flushed, vacuum-sealed in UV-blocking mylar foils, and stored at -10°F. Grains are custom-milled to order on the morning of dispatch, and live liquid yeast packs are shipped in insulated cold packaging with reusable ice packs.
Yes! We rent all-grain brewing rigs, counter-flow plate chillers, oxygenation kits, and commercial-grade CO2 draft equipment by the day or weekend. We also host private group brewing sessions for team building, birthdays, and private parties for up to 12 people.
Orders submitted by 2:00 PM PST ship out the same business day. Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days across the continental US, and cold-chain priority is available for perishable yeast. All orders over $75 qualify for complimentary standard shipping.
We love tasting community brews! Stop by during shop hours or join our Thursday Open Tap session. Our brew team will taste your beer, evaluate clarity and off-flavors, and suggest actionable adjustments for your recipe and process.
We stand behind every pint with our 100% Brew Guarantee. If an ingredient is defective or your package arrives compromised, reach out within 30 days. We'll troubleshoot the issue with you and send a free replacement kit or full refund.
