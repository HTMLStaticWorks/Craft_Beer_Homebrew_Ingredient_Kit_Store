Our Story

Born in a Garage. Built for Every Brewer.

From a beat-up kettle and a bag of pale malt in 2012, Supply Co. grew into the Pacific Northwest's most beloved homebrewing community and supply hub.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Everything we do is in service of one goal: making great beer more accessible to every passionate brewer.

Our Mission

To source and supply the freshest, highest-quality brewing ingredients while fostering a community where knowledge flows as freely as the beer. We exist to lower the barrier between a great idea and a great pint.

Our Vision

A world where every kitchen has a fermentation vessel, every home has a homebrew on tap, and the craft of brewing is passed from neighbor to neighbor like a recipe card — freely and joyfully.

2012 — Today

The Supply Co. Story

It started with a conversation between two friends over a mediocre commercial lager. "We can make better than this." That bet turned into a 5-gallon batch of pale ale brewed in a Portland garage — and never looked back.

Within two years, the garage became a storefront, the storefront became an institution, and the institution became a movement. Today, Supply Co. serves brewers across 38 states, stocks over 320 individual ingredients, and hosts weekly brewing workshops from our flagship location in Portland, OR.

We've never had a venture capital round, a private equity buyout, or a corporate parent. We're homebrewers who built a shop for homebrewers. Full stop.
