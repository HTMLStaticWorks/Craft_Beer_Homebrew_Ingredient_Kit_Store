Our Story

Born in a Garage. Built for Brewers.

Supply Co. started with a beat-up kettle, a bag of pale malt, and an obsession with great beer. What began as a weekend hobby in 2012 grew into the Pacific Northwest's most-loved homebrewing community.

Today we supply over 4,800 homebrewers with hand-sourced ingredients, curated equipment, and brewing education that goes far beyond a recipe card.

Hand-sourced ingredients — direct relationships with hop farms and maltsters.
Expert-led classes — taught by BJCP-certified homebrewers.
Community-first — free recipe forum, monthly brew-alongs, and 24/7 chat support.
